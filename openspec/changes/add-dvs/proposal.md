# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.x · after 1.0**

## Why

Serato, Traktor, rekordbox and VirtualDJ all play digital files from timecode vinyl; Mixxx does it for free.

## What Changes

- Control a deck from Serato, Traktor or rekordbox timecode vinyl or CDs through an audio interface's inputs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `deck-transport`: decks follow timecode vinyl

## Out of Scope

- Hardware mixers' own DVS protocols.

## Impact

engine inputs and deck control; xwax-style decoder.
