# Proposal

Planned for: **v0.6 · Buy it from the artist** (moved from v0.5 at the 0.5.0 release)

## Why

Slicer and Freeze pad modes are on the pads of Serato, Traktor, rekordbox and VirtualDJ.

## What Changes

- SLICER: the next 8 beats (or a bar) split across the 8 pads.
- FREEZE: pads play short slices of the current beat, stuttering.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `performance-pads`: SLICER and FREEZE pad pages

## Out of Scope

- Sequencer patterns (later).

## Impact

engine deck playback, pad pages, controller pad_panel values.
