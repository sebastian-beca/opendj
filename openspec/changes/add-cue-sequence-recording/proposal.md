# Proposal

Planned for: **v2.0** (moved from v0.5 at the 0.5.0 release, then from v0.6 on 2026-11-06)

## Why

Serato Flip-style recording of hot cue presses makes edits and extended intros on the fly.

## What Changes

- Record a sequence of hot cue jumps and censor presses on a deck, save it with the track, and play it back in time.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `deck-transport`: record and replay hot-cue sequences

## Out of Scope

- Editing sequences on a timeline.

## Impact

engine deck, library schema, deck UI.
