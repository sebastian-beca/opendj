# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.x · after 1.0**

## Why

Streaming a set live is common; VirtualDJ and Serato support it.

## What Changes

- Broadcast the master to Icecast/Shoutcast and to RTMP services (Twitch, YouTube, Mixcloud Live).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `mix-recording`: live broadcast of the master

## Out of Scope

- Video streaming beyond a still image.

## Impact

engine output tap, encoder dependencies.
