# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.x · after 1.0**

## Why

The sampler only has eight built-in sounds; every app lets DJs load their own and record from a deck.

## What Changes

- Load any audio file into a sampler pad, banks of 8, and record a loop from a deck into a pad.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `performance-pads`: own samples, banks and recording

## Out of Scope

- A sample editor.

## Impact

engine sampler, library.
