# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.0 · Ready for any DJ switching over**

## Why

VirtualDJ and Mixxx users expect to adjust the screen beyond the fixed layouts.

## What Changes

- Show or hide deck sections (pads, FX, loop), and choose waveform height, per layout.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `app-layout`: per-layout section visibility

## Out of Scope

- Full skin files.

## Impact

app UI.
