## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Supported controllers
Controllers on openDJ's supported list SHALL have a checked mapping, a layout drawing in the Controller Editor and working pad modes and lights, and SHALL be marked Supported in Settings › Controllers.

#### Scenario: Plug in a DDJ-400
- **WHEN** a DDJ-400 is connected
- **THEN** it is marked Supported, works without setup, and the Controller Editor shows its drawing
