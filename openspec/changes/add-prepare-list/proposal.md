# Proposal

Planned for: **v0.7 · Your library, everywhere**

## Why

Planning a set without loading decks: a prepare list (rekordbox) or practice mode keeps tracks to look at before a gig.

## What Changes

- A Prepare tab in the side panel, with prep status per track and a short preview.
- Practice mode: mark tracks as practised and see transitions between consecutive tracks by key and tempo.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `track-browser`: Prepare tab and practice marks

## Out of Scope

- Cloud sync of prepare lists.

## Impact

app side panel.
