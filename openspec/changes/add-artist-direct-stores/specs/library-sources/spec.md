## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Just bought
Tracks arriving from a purchase SHALL appear in a Just bought list, analysed and tagged, until moved on.

#### Scenario: Purchase lands
- **WHEN** a purchase arrives through the inbox
- **THEN** it is in Just bought with tempo and key

### Requirement: More stores
Each artist-direct store with a usable search SHALL be a store add-on installable from the official repository.

#### Scenario: Install Mirlo
- **WHEN** the Mirlo add-on is installed
- **THEN** Mirlo results appear in store search
