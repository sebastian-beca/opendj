# Proposal

Planned for: **v0.5 · Buy it from the artist**

## Why

More artist-direct stores mean more money to artists and more chance a track is found.

## What Changes

- Store add-ons for Mirlo, Ampwall and Faircamp sites where they offer search or download.
- A "Just bought" list: purchases land analysed and tagged in one place.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-sources`: more artist-direct store add-ons and a Just bought list

## Out of Scope

- Stores without a search or link scheme.

## Impact

New add-ons, store search API.
