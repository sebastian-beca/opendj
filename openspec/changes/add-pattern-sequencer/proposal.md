# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.x · after 1.0**

## Why

rekordbox and Traktor have sequencers playing samples in patterns under the mix.

## What Changes

- A 16-step sequencer per sampler bank, synced to the master tempo.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `performance-pads`: step sequencer

## Out of Scope

- Piano roll.

## Impact

engine sampler.
