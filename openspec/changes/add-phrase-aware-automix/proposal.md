# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.x · after 1.0**

## Why

Automix mixes by time today; phrases and VirtualDJ's automix points let it mix intro over outro.

## What Changes

- Automix starts the mix at the outgoing track's outro and brings in the next track's intro, using phrases or imported automix points.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `automix`: phrase-aware mixing

## Out of Scope

- Stem-based transitions.

## Impact

app automix.
