## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Search everywhere
A search SHALL be runnable across the Collection, Files and store sources at once, with results grouped by source.

#### Scenario: Everywhere
- **WHEN** the DJ searches an artist with Everywhere on
- **THEN** results from the collection and each store show in groups

### Requirement: Jump and show
The track menu SHALL offer Go to artist, album and label, and Show in playlists.

#### Scenario: Show in playlists
- **WHEN** Show in playlists is chosen
- **THEN** the lists holding the track are listed
