# Proposal

Planned for: **v0.6 · Buy it from the artist** (moved from v0.5 at the 0.5.0 release)

## Why

The headline use of stems in 2026 is echoing out a vocal, playing an acapella or an instrumental. Stems mute and level today but take no effects.

## What Changes

- Per-stem quick effect (echo out, filter) as Mixxx's `[QuickEffectRack1_[ChannelN_StemM]]`.
- Acapella and Instrumental one-press pads.
- STEMS knob mode fully mapped on the test controllers.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `stems`: stem effects and acapella / instrumental pads

## Out of Scope

- Stem-separated sampler recording.

## Impact

engine stems and effects routing, deck.slint STEMS page, controller mappings.
