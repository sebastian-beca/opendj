# Proposal

Planned for: **v1.x · after 1.0**

## Why

Suggesting what to play next by key, tempo, energy and history helps any DJ.

## What Changes

- A Suggestions tab ranking tracks that mix with the playing one, using key, tempo, energy, history and links.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `track-browser`: suggestions tab

## Out of Scope

- Cloud recommendations.

## Impact

library query, app side panel.
