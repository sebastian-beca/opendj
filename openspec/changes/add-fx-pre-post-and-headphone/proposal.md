# Proposal

Planned for: **v0.6 · Stems and performance**

## Why

Previewing an effect before the crowd hears it, and choosing pre- or post-fader effects, are standard on club mixers.

## What Changes

- Each deck FX unit can be pre- or post-fader.
- A headphone-only mode: the effect is heard in the cue mix only until switched to the master.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `effects`: pre/post-fader and headphone-only FX

## Out of Scope

- Third-party plugins (v2.0).

## Impact

engine effects routing, mixer UI.
