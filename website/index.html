Switching is easy

Bring your library from rekordbox, VirtualDJ or Mixxx.

No exporting, no starting over. openDJ reads the other app's library where it already lives, so your prep comes with you: tracks, hot cues, loops, beat grids, playlists and history.

  1. rekordbox

    rekordbox 6 and 7's own library, or an XML export from rekordbox 5. Hot cues, memory cues, grids with their tempo changes, phrases, playlists, intelligent playlists and history.

  2. VirtualDJ

    Your VirtualDJ database: tracks, cues, playlists, folders and history, with a step-by-step dialog that can take the import back out again.

  3. Mixxx

    Mixxx's library, read while Mixxx stays open: hot cues with labels and colours, saved loops, grids, keys, playlists, crates and history.

Pick the lists you want in a tree, with Select all. The same song in two apps becomes one track in openDJ, and your other app's library is never changed. Settings → Collection → Import, or the guided tour on first start. How importing works →

Automix

Let Automix take the next one.

  1. Queue your tracks. Drag them into the Automix list, or right-click any track and add it.
  2. Pick a length and a style. Short cuts or long, smooth blends, in seconds or bars.
  3. Press Automix. openDJ beatmatches and crossfades through the list while you step away, or jump in at any time.

Layouts

Room for the decks, or room for the music.

2-Deck gives you the full decks, pads and effects. 4-Deck puts four decks side by side, djay style. Simple shrinks the decks to one row so the browser gets the screen, and a compact view hides the decks altogether.

Controllers

Plug in your controller.

openDJ runs Mixxx's controller mappings, so more than 140 MIDI controllers work as soon as they're plugged in. The Controller Editor shows what every control does, and lets you change it in your own copy of a mapping.

Everything a mix needs.

  1. 01

    Stems

    Every track splits into vocals, drums, bass and the rest as it loads, ready on the pads. Studio-quality stems on request.

  2. 02

    Beat grids and phrases

    Grids that follow real tempo changes, and intro, build, drop and outro marked under each deck.

  3. 03

    Key mixing

    Key lock, harmonic or fuzzy matching, and tracks shifted to fit as they load.

  4. 04

    Effects and sampler

    Beat-synced effects in their own FX bar, Color FX on every filter knob, and 16 sampler pads.

  5. 05

    Collection Health

    Merge duplicates, clean up names and genres, fill in tags and covers from online, all with undo.

  6. 06

    Sets and crates

    Plan a gig as a set from staging to the live set, and keep your own crates beside it.

  7. 07

    Streaming playlists

    Browse your Spotify and TIDAL playlists, load the tracks you own, and copy a playlist into openDJ.

  8. 08

    Record your mix

    WAV or FLAC, with a tracklist and every move you made logged next to it.

  9. 09

    Ableton Link

    Play in time with other apps and devices on your network.

Under the hood

Rust, end to end.

openDJ is a ground-up rewrite in Rust that follows Mixxx's control names and controller mapping format, so Mixxx mappings run unchanged. It keeps its own collection and doesn't read Mixxx's database. The interface is built with Slint, and the waveforms are drawn with wgpu shaders. It's free software under the GPL, version 2 or later.
