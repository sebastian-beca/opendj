Built to play

Everything you need. Nothing in the way.

Nine things openDJ does well, and keeps getting better at.

01

Fast, clean, and made to play.

A calm interface that responds the moment you touch it. Sharp colour waveforms, drawn on your graphics card, show every kick, snare and vocal coming.

  • Colour waveforms with intro, build, drop and outro marked
  • 2-Deck, 4-Deck and Simple layouts
  • Written in Rust, light on your laptop
02

Your collection, cleaned up.

openDJ analyzes every track and shows how ready your collection is. Fix the sure things in one go, then review the rest one card at a time.

  • Duplicates, missing files and strays found for you
  • Names, tags, genres and covers filled in from online
  • Every fix can be undone
03

All your music, one library.

Your files, your playlists on Spotify and TIDAL, and your music server, side by side in one browser. Streaming services are browse-only: see your playlists, and play the tracks you own.

  • Spotify, TIDAL, Apple Music, SoundCloud and Mixcloud
  • Bandcamp and Beatport for buying what's missing
  • Plex and other music servers
04

Studio-quality stems.

Every track splits into vocals, drums, bass and the rest as it loads. When it matters, prepare high-quality stems for the cleanest acapellas and instrumentals.

  • Stem pads on every deck
  • Acapella or instrumental in one tap
  • Runs on your computer, no cloud needed
05

Beat grids that follow the music.

A neural beat tracker finds every beat and bar one. Steady tracks get a straight grid, and live drummers and tempo changes get a grid that bends with them.

  • Dynamic grids for real tempo changes
  • Lock a grid once it's right
  • Bar counter, phrases and a metronome
06

Mix in key without thinking about it.

Every track shows whether it fits the one playing. Tracks a semitone away are marked, and can shift to fit as they load.

  • Harmonic or fuzzy key matching
  • Key lock on by default
  • A filter for tracks that mix with this one
07

Automix, in your pocket.

Queue the tracks and openDJ beatmatches and blends them for you. Scan a QR code and run the queue from your phone, with nothing to install.

  • Blends in bars or seconds, smooth or quick
  • Search, add and reorder from your phone
  • Sleep timer to end the night
08

Play instruments over your mix.

Drop a synth or the Groove Box drum machine on a deck. Play it from your pads, a MIDI keyboard or the sequencer, in time and in key with the track.

  • VST3, CLAP and Audio Unit instruments and effects
  • Groove Box with classic drum kits
  • Scale, chord and arpeggiator modes
09

Plug in your controller.

More than 140 controllers work through Mixxx's mappings, and a growing list gets openDJ's own care. Change any control in the Controller Editor, or teach it a new one.

  • Pioneer DDJ-FLX4, FLX6, XDJ-RX3, DDJ-400 and more
  • Traktor Kontrol S2, S3 and S4, Numark V7, Xone:K2
  • MIDI learn for anything without a mapping

Frequently asked questions.

Is openDJ really free?

Yes. openDJ is open source under the GPL, with no subscription, no paid tier and no account. If it plays a part in your sets, you can buy us a coffee.

Will my controller work?

Most likely. openDJ runs Mixxx's mappings for more than 140 MIDI and HID controllers, and the most popular ones get their own mapping and a drawing in the Controller Editor. If yours misbehaves, tell us which one.

Can I bring my rekordbox, VirtualDJ or Mixxx library?

Yes. openDJ reads the other app's library where it lives, with hot cues, loops, grids, playlists and history. Your other app's library is never changed, so you can keep using both. How importing works →

Is it ready to play a gig?

openDJ is in public beta. Plenty of sets are played on it, but play yours at home first on your own laptop, audio interface and controller, and tell us what you find.
