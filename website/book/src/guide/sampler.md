# Sampler

The **SAMPLER** pad page plays short sounds over the mix: drops, horns,
rewinds, name tags. There are 16 pads, eight at a time: the **1** and **2**
in the narrow column left of the pads switch between them. On a DDJ-FLX4 the
pads in SAMPLER mode play the same eight the screen shows.

## Sample packs

**SAMPLE PACK** under the pads picks a pack. openDJ comes with the
**Default DJ Sample Pack**; **+** makes a new, empty one.

What you change on a pad is kept for that pack, and the pack's own files are
left alone.

Under the list of packs:

- **Export** saves the pads as they are now, with their names, levels and
  play modes, as a `.opendjpack` file in your Downloads folder, and shows it.
  Send it to another DJ, or keep it as a backup.
- **Import…** adds a `.opendjpack` as a new pack and plays it. Dropping one
  on a pad does the same.
- **Rename…** and **Delete Pack** work on the pack you play (not the
  Default one). Delete asks for a second click, and moves the pack to a
  `.deleted` folder beside the others rather than erasing it.
- **Show Folder** opens the pack's folder (for the Default pack, the folder
  all packs live in).

## Your own sounds

- **Drop an audio file** (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A or OGG, up to a minute)
  from Finder, Explorer or the track list onto a pad, or right-click a pad
  and choose **Load File…**.
- **Choose Sample…** borrows a sound from any pack, with a play button to
  hear it in the headphones first.

## Changing a pad

The **pencil** shows a level knob and a **•••** menu on each pad. The menu
(or a right-click) renames the pad, sets how it plays (**One shot**,
**Hold**, **On/Off** or **Loop**), puts back the pack's own sound, or
clears it.

Drag a pad onto another to swap them. Shift-click a pad to stop it.
Turning a pad's level while it plays glides, so it doesn't crackle.
