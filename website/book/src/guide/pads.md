# Pads

Each deck has eight pads, with tabs on top that switch what they do. The
tabs follow the pad mode buttons of a Pioneer DDJ-FLX4, so the screen and
the controller always show the same page.

## Pad pages

Four tabs show at a time. The first four are **HOT CUES**, **PAD FX 1**,
**BEAT JUMP** and **SAMPLER**. **Shift-click** a tab for its partner, as
SHIFT + a pad mode button does on the controller: **KEYBOARD**, **PAD FX
2**, **BEAT LOOP** and **KEY SHIFT**.

**Right-click** a tab to put another page in it. There are ten pages in all;
**STEMS** and **LOOP ROLL** are only there this way. For example, put STEMS
in place of the sampler. A controller's pad mode button then opens whatever
page is in its tab.

The narrow column left of the pads flips the open page's own pages, such as
the sampler's **1** and **2**.

| Page | Pads |
|---|---|
| **HOT CUES** | Eight hot cues |
| **PAD FX 1** | Echo, reverb, filters, flanger and more, while held (see [Effects](fx.md#pad-fx)) |
| **BEAT JUMP** | Jump back or forward 4, 8, 16 or 32 beats |
| **SAMPLER** | 16 short sounds, eight at a time (see [Sampler](sampler.md)) |
| **STEMS** | Mute and solo the track's stems (see [Stems](stems.md#stems-pads)) |
| **BEAT LOOP** | Loops from 1/4 to 32 beats (see [Looping](looping.md#beat-loop-pads)) |
| **PAD FX 2** | Roll, gate, echo out, brake and more, while held |
| **KEYBOARD** | Play from a hot cue at another key |
| **KEY SHIFT** | Shift the track's key |
| **LOOP ROLL** | Loops while held, from 1/16 to 8 beats (see [Looping](looping.md#loop-rolls)) |

## Hot cues

A hot cue marks a place in the track to jump back to.

- **Press an empty pad** (it shows **+**) to set a hot cue where the track is
  playing.
- **Press a set pad** to jump there and play.
- **Shift-click a pad** to delete its cue.

The eight hot cues are blue, green, yellow, orange, red, pink, purple and
teal, and show in the same colour on the waveform. A saved loop shows a loop
sign instead of a play mark. Hot cues from VirtualDJ come in with their names
and colours, and are written to the stick in a
[USB export](usb-export.md).

## Keyboard and key shift

**KEYBOARD** plays from the deck's hot cue at another key, like a sampler
keyboard: the pads go 0, +1, +2, +3 semitones on the top row and −4, −3,
−2, −1 on the bottom. They take the colour of the hot cue they play.

**KEY SHIFT** sets the track's key shift: 0, +1, +2, +3 on top and −3, −2,
−1, 0 below. Shift-click a pad to move the key up or down by its step
instead. A pad is lit while the deck plays at its key.

See [Keys](decks.md#keys) for key lock and key matching.
