# Ableton Link

Ableton Link plays openDJ in time with other apps and devices on your
network, such as Ableton Live, a drum machine app or another DJ.

Open the **MASTER** button in the top bar and turn on **Ableton Link**.

Link follows the master deck: the deck that's playing, or the one started first
while both play (see [Sync and quantize](decks.md#sync-and-quantize)).
