# Automix

Automix mixes through a list of tracks for you.

1. **Queue your tracks.** Drag tracks into the Automix panel on the right, or
   right-click a track and choose **Add to Automix**.
2. **Pick a length and a style.** Short cuts or long, smooth blends.
3. **Press Automix.** openDJ beatmatches and crossfades through the list.

You can take over at any time.
