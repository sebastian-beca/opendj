## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SB3 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Decks 3 and 4 only work
while at least one output goes to the DDJ-SB3's sound card.

- **Pad modes:** **HOT CUE** (SHIFT: beat jump), **FX FADE** (SHIFT: roll),
  **PAD SCRATCH** (SHIFT: slicer) and **SAMPLER**.
- **DECK 3** and **DECK 4** switch each side to its second deck.
- **FX 1-3** turn the effects of that side's unit on and off, and **FX
  LEVEL** sets how much you hear.
- **VINYL** turns scratching on the jog wheel on and off. SHIFT and
  **VINYL** is slip mode.
- The **MASTER** and **HEADPHONES** level knobs work on the controller's own
  sound card, not on openDJ's knobs.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the lights follow what's on screen on decks 1-4.
