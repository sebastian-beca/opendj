## Getting started

Plug the V7 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself. One
V7 plays one deck: set its deck switch to **A** for deck 1 or **B** for
deck 2. With two V7s, set one to each.

The mapping is Mixxx's, written in 2012. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a
real V7 yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The platter** spins with the V7's own motor, and the track follows it
  as a record would: hand on it to scratch, let go to play on. **MOTOR OFF**
  stops the motor and turns the platter into a jog wheel. **START** and
  **STOP** set how fast the motor spins up and brakes.
- Holding **SHIFT** and pressing **MOTOR OFF** turns scratching off while
  the motor runs.
- **PLAY**, **CUE** and **SYNC** do what they say. **REVERSE** plays
  backwards while it's on.
- **PITCH** has a fine and a coarse half, so it moves in very small steps.
  **RANGE** steps through 8, 10, 16, 50 and 100%. **KEY LOCK** keeps the key
  as the tempo changes, and **PITCH BEND −/+** nudge the tempo while held.
- **HOT CUE 1-5** set and play hot cues. Press **SHIFT** (it stays lit),
  then a hot cue, to delete it.
- **Loops:** **LOOP MODE** switches between manual loops (**LOOP IN**,
  **LOOP OUT**, **RELOOP**) and auto loops of 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats on the
  same buttons. **LOOP ½** and **×2** halve and double a loop, and
  **RELOOP/EXIT** leaves it or goes back into it.
- **STRIP SEARCH** jumps through the track.
- **TAP** sets the track's BPM when tapped in time.
- **The FX section** plays a flanger on the deck, as Mixxx did before 2.0:
  **FX ON** turns it on, **FX AMOUNT** sets its depth, **FX PARAM** its
  speed and **FX SELECT** its delay (press to reset). It plays through the
  deck's Pad FX, so holding a Pad FX pad at the same time takes it over.
- **BROWSE** scrolls the library, the list buttons step through playlists,
  and **LOAD A** / **LOAD B** load the selected track.

## When you try it

- Play a track with the motor on and check it stays in time with the
  platter, then scratch it.
- Turn the motor off and nudge and scratch with the platter as a jog wheel.
- Try both deck switch positions, and two V7s if you have them.
- Set, play and delete hot cues; try manual and auto loops.
- Turn on the FX and move its controls.
- Check the lights follow what's on screen and turn off when you quit.
