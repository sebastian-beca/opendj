## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-400 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The DDJ-400 works much like the DDJ-FLX4 that came after it, so most of
what's on the [FLX4 page](pioneer-ddj-flx4.md) applies here too. The pads
have four pages: **HOT CUE**, **BEAT LOOP**, **BEAT JUMP** and **SAMPLER**.
The **FILTER** knobs run each deck's filter, and the **BEAT FX** section on
the right of the mixer runs the Beat FX.

## When you try it

- Load a track with **LOAD**, play it, and scratch and nudge it with the jog
  wheel.
- Try every pad page, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the pad, PLAY and CUE lights follow what's on screen.
- Move the **BEAT FX** channel lever through CH1, CH2 and MASTER.
