## Getting started

Plug the Mixtrack Pro FX in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the
main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

- **Pad modes:** **CUE** (hot cues; SHIFT: beat jump), **AUTO LOOP** (hold
  SHIFT: loop roll), **FADER CUTS** and **SAMPLE** (SHIFT: the second bank of
  samples). The bottom four pads do what the top four do.
- Either **SHIFT** shifts both decks.
- **SCRATCH** is on at the start. SHIFT and **SCRATCH** plays backwards
  while held.
- The six **FX** buttons step to the previous or next effect, whatever is
  printed on them. Each **FX** paddle turns its side's effect on.
- **PITCH BEND −/+** nudge the tempo; SHIFT and **−** changes the tempo
  range, SHIFT and **+** turns key lock on.

## When you try it

- Play, scratch and nudge both decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Step through effects and turn them on with the paddles.
