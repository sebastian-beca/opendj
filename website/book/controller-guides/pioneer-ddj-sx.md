## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SX in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. This is Mixxx's mapping;
the DDJ-SX2 and DDJ-RX have their own entries that use it too.

- **DECK 1-4** switch each side between its two decks; the four mixer
  channels are, left to right, 3, 1, 2 and 4.
- **Pad modes:** **HOT CUE**, **ROLL**, **SLICER** and **SAMPLER**. Hold
  **SAMPLER** to switch the sampler pads to velocity. **PARAMETER ◀ ▶**
  change the beat jump size, the roll lengths or the slicer range.
- **FX1** and **FX2** run effect units 1 and 2; the **FX ASSIGN** buttons on
  each channel send it to them (SHIFT: units 3 and 4).
- **VINYL** turns scratching on the jog wheel on and off. **KEYLOCK** turns
  key lock on; hold it to reset the tempo.
- The **MASTER** and **HEADPHONES** level knobs work on the controller's own
  sound card, not on openDJ's knobs.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the lights follow what's on screen on decks 1-4.
