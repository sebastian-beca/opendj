## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-FLX4 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

- **Pads** play the pad page shown on screen: hot cues, pad FX, beat jump,
  sampler, keyboard, beat loop, key shift and loop roll. SHIFT and a pad
  mode button picks the page printed under it.
- **CFX** knobs run each deck's Color FX, and the **BEAT FX** section the
  Beat FX.
- SHIFT and the right **LOAD** changes the layout; SHIFT and the left
  **LOAD** shows linked tracks.
- The tempo fader goes slower when pushed up, as on Pioneer gear. Turn on
  **Tempo fader direction** in **Settings › Decks** to change that.
