## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SX2 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The SX2 sends the same MIDI as the DDJ-SX, so it runs Mixxx's DDJ-SX
mapping (see the [DDJ-SX guide](pioneer-ddj-sx.md)). **CAPTURE** and the
sequencer buttons (**OVERDUB**, **SEQ START**) do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the lights follow what's on screen on decks 1-4.
