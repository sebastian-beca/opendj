## Getting started

Plug the Mixtrack Platinum FX in with its USB cable and it picks this
mapping by itself. Update its firmware to version 1.10 or later first (from
macOS or Windows), or some controls won't work. It's also a sound card:
choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main output on channels 1-2 and
the headphones on 3-4.

- **Jog wheel screens** show the deck, the platter position, the BPM, key
  lock, the tempo range and change, and the time left.
- **Pad modes:** **CUE** (hot cues), **AUTO LOOP**, **FADER CUTS** and
  **SAMPLE**. SHIFT and a pad mode gives its second page.
- SHIFT and **SCRATCH** switches each side between decks 1 and 3, or 2 and
  4.
- The six **FX** buttons turn effects on (SHIFT for more than one at a
  time), the **FX** paddle latches up or holds down, and **BEATS** sets the
  effect's length.
- **TAP** taps the tempo of the deck whose headphone **CUE** is on.

## When you try it

- Check that the jog wheel screens show the BPM, the tempo and the time
  left while a track plays.
- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode and the six FX buttons.
