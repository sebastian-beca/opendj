## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-RX in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The RX sends the same MIDI as the DDJ-SX2, with rekordbox's labels, so it
runs Mixxx's DDJ-SX mapping (see the [DDJ-SX guide](pioneer-ddj-sx.md)) with
these changes:

- **QUANTIZE** turns quantize on and off for the deck.
- **PAD FX** mode plays loop rolls, as the SX's ROLL mode does.
- **MASTER TEMPO** is key lock. SHIFT and **SLIP** turns scratching on the
  jog wheel on and off.
- The **COLOR** knobs are filters. The Sound Color FX buttons (**NOISE**,
  **CRUSH**, **PITCH**, **FILTER**), **CAPTURE** and the sequencer buttons do
  nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that QUANTIZE and the other lights follow what's on screen.
