## Getting started

Plug the Inpulse 500 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the
main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Its master and
headphone knobs work on the controller's sound card directly.

- **Pad modes** (eight): hot cue, loop, slicer, sampler, toneplay, roll, FX
  and beat jump. SHIFT and a pad gives that mode's second page.
- **FX 1-4** pick which deck each side plays: 1 and 3 on the left, 2 and 4
  on the right.
- The **LOOP** encoder halves or doubles the loop when turned and turns it
  on or off when pressed. SHIFT and a press sets a 4-beat loop.
- **ASSISTANT** turns Automix on or off.
- **QUANT** turns quantize on; SHIFT and **QUANT** is key lock.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks, switching with **FX 1-4**.
- Try all eight pad modes, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the pad and button lights follow what's on screen.
