## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-800 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from Pioneer's MIDI message list. The
DDJ-800 numbers its decks, pads and mixer like the DDJ-FLX4, so it shares
the FLX4's mapping and adds its own buttons:

- **Pad modes:** **HOT CUE**, **PAD FX**, **BEAT JUMP** and **SAMPLER**;
  with SHIFT, **KEYBOARD**, **PAD FX 2**, **BEAT LOOP** and **KEY SHIFT**.
- **QUANTIZE**, **MASTER TEMPO** (key lock) and **KEY SYNC** (SHIFT: reset
  the key). **TEMPO RANGE** steps through the tempo ranges.
- **SEARCH ◀ ▶** jump a beat, and keep going while held.
- **Beat FX:** **CH SELECT** picks the channel (or MASTER), **FX SELECT**
  loads the closest openDJ effect (MT DELAY plays Echo, TRANS plays Gate,
  ENIGMA JET plays Flanger, PITCH plays Chorus, the MOBIUS ones play
  Ladder), then **LEVEL/DEPTH** and **ON/OFF**.
- The jog displays show the BPM, the elapsed time and the playhead going
  round.
- Not mapped yet: **MEMORY**, **DELETE**, the pad **PAGE** buttons and the
  **COLOR FX** buttons. The **COLOR FX** knobs work as filters.

## When you try it

- Play, scratch and loop on both decks.
- Turn **CH SELECT** and **FX SELECT** and check the Beat FX on screen.
- Check what the jog displays show; their numbers were written from
  Pioneer's list without a controller to try them on.
