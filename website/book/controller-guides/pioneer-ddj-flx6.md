## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-FLX6 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Use the **4-Deck** layout
to see all four decks on screen.

This mapping is openDJ's own, written from Pioneer's list of the FLX6's MIDI
messages and built on the [DDJ-FLX4 mapping](pioneer-ddj-flx4.md), so the
two work the same way where they share controls.

- **DECK 1/3** and **DECK 2/4** pick the deck each side plays. The mixer has
  all four channels, each with its own trim, EQ, filter, cue and fader.
- **Pads** play the pad page shown on screen, as on the FLX4. SHIFT and a pad
  mode button picks the page printed under it.
- **BEAT FX** (FX1 and FX2 on the FLX6) runs openDJ's Beat FX: any ON/OFF
  turns it on, **FX SELECT** steps the effect (SHIFT: backwards), **BEAT ◀ ▶**
  halve and double its length (SHIFT: auto and tap tempo) and **CH SELECT**
  sends it to one channel or, on MST, all four.
- **MERGE FX** runs the deck FX of the deck that side plays: turn it to raise
  them, press it to turn them on and off, and **PRESET** steps the chain.
- **MASTER** makes the deck the one the others sync to.
- The jog rings show the playhead going round, as on a record.
- **VIEW** changes the layout, **BACK** moves between the track list and the
  sidebar.

Not mapped yet: JOG CUTTER, CUE/LOOP MEMORY, MASTER CUE and Serato's
SP. SCRATCH.

## When you try it

- Load a track on each of the four decks with **LOAD 1** to **LOAD 4**.
- Switch each side between its two decks and check PLAY, CUE, the pads and
  the jog wheel follow.
- Move every mixer channel's faders and knobs, and the level meters.
- Try every pad page, with and without SHIFT.
- Turn on the Beat FX and move **CH SELECT** through CH1 to CH4 and MST.
- Turn and press **MERGE FX** on both sides.
- Watch the jog rings while a track plays.
