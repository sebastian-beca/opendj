## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-200 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
The DDJ-200 has no sound card of its own, so headphone cueing needs a second
output on your computer, such as a USB headphone adapter, chosen in
**Settings › Audio**.

- **Pads** play hot cues 1-8. SHIFT and a pad clears that hot cue.
- **SHIFT** (left) and a **HEADPHONE CUE** button loads the selected track
  onto that deck.
- **SHIFT** (right) and a **HEADPHONE CUE** button switches that side
  between decks 1 and 3, or 2 and 4. The button lights while deck 3 or 4 is
  active.
- **MASTER** moves the headphone mix between cue and main. SHIFT and
  **MASTER** turns four decks on or off.
- **TRANSITION FX** turns Automix on or off.

## When you try it

- Load, play and scratch on both decks, then on decks 3 and 4.
- Set and clear hot cues with the pads.
- Check that Automix starts and stops with **TRANSITION FX**.
