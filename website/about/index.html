About

Built with love in Oakland, CA.

openDJ is made by DJ Nahuel, a DJ who wanted software that's fast, honest and free, for the love of playing music.

Why openDJ

DJ software has drifted towards subscriptions, accounts and cloud plans. openDJ goes the other way: every feature is free, your music stays your own files, and stems and beat detection run on your own computer. No ads, and no hidden AI plan.

It's made for every kind of night: mixing at home, sharing a set with friends, or bringing the neighborhood together.

How it's built

openDJ is a ground-up rewrite in Rust. It follows Mixxx's control names and controller mapping format, so more than 140 Mixxx controller mappings run unchanged. The interface is built with Slint, and the waveforms are drawn on your graphics card with wgpu. It's free software under the GPL, version 2 or later.

openDJ stands on the shoulders of open-source work: the Mixxx project and its mapping authors, and the many libraries listed in the source code. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Mixxx project.

Get involved

openDJ is in public beta and grows with the DJs who use it. Play it on your own gear, report what works and what doesn't, and vote on what comes next.
